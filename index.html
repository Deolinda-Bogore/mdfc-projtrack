Whole System Structure
PMS
Programs
Project management
  • Add projects from application or proposal work plans.
  • Assign tasks to project leads and implementors.
  • Track activities, timelines, outputs, remarks, challenges, and comments.
  • M&E evidence: data, testimonials, videos, attachments, indicators, and survey results.
  • Grants database: grant name, funder, owner, link, progress, and remarks.
Finance
Requisition and budget monitoring
  • Smart requisitions with automatic number, attachments, comments, and status.
  • Budget management: preparation, monitoring, forecasting, adjustments, and alerts.
  • Approval channel: request preparator, finance verification, executive/board approval.
  • Payments and cash advances with vouchers, receipts, and accountability.
  • Procurement and audit trail linked to project, donor, budget line, user, history, and documents.
Administration
Daily operations
  • Monthly operations: rent, taxes, subscriptions, amount, responsible person, due date, remarks.
  • Asset register: category, item, responsible person, contact, status, and documents.
  • Vendors and suppliers: category, name, contracts, timeline, telephone, account number.
  • HR and payroll: employee records, contracts, attendance, performance, and training.
  • Inventory and travel: office supplies, vehicles, travel records, renewals, and reports.
Golden Rule: every transaction links to a project, donor, budget line, user, approval history, supporting documents, and audit trail.
